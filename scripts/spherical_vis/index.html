Authoritative model provenance & frame treatment

Chemical types and reference plans are from the experiment’s perceive_monomer. Angular surfaces encode the output of frame_harmonics as exact degree ≤2 Cartesian polynomials, validated against independent directions. Pair sums are checked against Arm.energy; v4 predictions are checked against the published 528-record score payload.

v4 axial uses C10/C20 only. v5 C2v additionally uses C22c (Racah slots 0,3,6). Frames are averaged within each axial orbit over transverse references, then across distinct axial references. Missing transverse references do not invent an x axis. The angular coefficients are bounded per degree by ρ=0.40; a20 and a22c share the same l=2 norm budget, guaranteeing f ≥ 0.20.

Aromatic v4 carbon has z toward H in the ring plane: π-face and in-plane perpendicular values are equal. C22c can break that degeneracy in v5; no v5 result is fabricated when its checkpoint is absent. Switching to one reference frame displays an individual contribution, never an alternative learned energy.








What is being visualized? Frame conventions & provenance

These are angular basis functions, not fitted exchange energies, electron densities, or orbitals.
The lobe radius is scale × |Cₗₘ|; hue indicates sign. Racah normalization:
Cₗₘ = √(4π/(2l+1)) Yₗₘ, positive Cartesian convention from apnet_pt/mastiff.py.


No audited chemical frame assignments are stored on this branch. This viewer uses an explicitly
visualization-only heuristic: monomer-local bonds from 1.2× covalent radii, iterative
element/neighborhood graph ranks for z then x reference roles, all tied assignments retained.
Plans are frozen at 1.0 Rₑ for all eight geometries; the dimer partner never chooses a reference.
Terminal and wholly collinear sites are axial (10/20 only); isolated sites are explicitly isotropic.


Full frames use z toward the reference, x the second bond projected perpendicular to z,
y = z × x. Equivalent-frame mode averages harmonic values, not surfaces, and suppresses sine channels
as in AveragedFrameHarmonics. The one-frame inspection mode shows one tied assignment
(not a permutation-invariant prediction) and enables sine channels; axial sites still have no transverse gauge.
Axes show all equivalent reference frames in average mode. Vanishing or masked channels have no lobe.
